Who We Are

A Community of Love & Power

Welcome to RCCG Liveliest People Cathedral (Kwara Province 3 Trinity Family), a vibrant fellowship where every soul is cherished and every gift is celebrated. Under the leadership of Pastor Olaniyan Joshua A. Tosin (APICR Admin Reg 4 / PICP Kwara Province 3), we are dedicated to experiencing God's presence and manifesting His love in our world.

Whether you're visiting for the first time or looking for a home, you’ll find a warm welcome and a life-transforming experience right here in Ilorin.
